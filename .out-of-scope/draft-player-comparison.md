# Side-by-side player comparison in the Draft room

A Captain can't pick two or more players from the pool and compare their stats and signup answers side by side.

## Why this is out of scope

The Draft happens once per Bingo, a short session every few months. For it, a Captain already has each player's profile (stats and signup answers), can scout the signups from the moment they open, and has their own private Pick Ratings to rank players ahead of time. Comparison happens while scouting, which has days, not in the draft room, where each pick is on the clock.

A useful comparison view isn't just two profiles placed next to each other. It needs decisions on which stats line up, how to compare free-text signup answers, how it works on a phone, and how it fits the draft room's pick flow. That's a design project of its own, for a tool used once per Bingo, when scouting and Pick Ratings already cover the need.

If it comes back, start from a design pass that shows what a Captain couldn't decide with profiles plus Pick Ratings.
