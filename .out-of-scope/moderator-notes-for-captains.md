# Moderator notes for Captains

A Moderator can't attach a note to a signup that Captains then see in the draft room (for example "confirmed alt" or "known duo").

## Why this is out of scope

What a Captain sees about a player in the draft room is the player's own signup answers and stats, plus the Captain's own private Pick Ratings. A Moderator-to-Captain note would add a third kind of information: something one role writes about a player for another role to act on during the draft. That needs its own rules for who can write, who can read, whether the player can see it, and whether it's audited. It would sit next to the moderator-only audit history without being part of it.

Moderators already handle the cases this was meant for with the tools they have, and they haven't asked for this. So the extra visibility tier isn't worth its rules and UI.

If it comes back, start from a concrete draft-room case the current tools couldn't handle.
